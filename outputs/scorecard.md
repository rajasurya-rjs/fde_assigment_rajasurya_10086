_Naive_ = share of all records with response < 10 min and no validation (impossible negative times count as passes). _Clean-record rate_ = the same share among records that pass every ERROR rule. **M1 is the clean-record rate**, published only when the evidence supports it:

- **MEETS / BELOW** (proven): the bounds lie entirely above / below 90%, so no assumption about the quarantined records can change the verdict.
- **MEETS / BELOW (estimate)**: the bounds straddle 90%; the verdict treats the quarantined records as out of scope, accepted only when ≥ 90% of records are clean (a policy line set equal to the standard's own 10% tolerance).
- **NOT MEASURABLE**: the bounds straddle 90% and fewer than 90% of records are clean. No verdict; request corrected data.
